About Heaven Restaurant

Heaven Restaurant & Boutique Hotel is one of Kigali's most beloved dining establishments, offering exceptional food with a meaningful mission. Founded by Josh and Alissa Ruxin in 2008, Heaven is not just a restaurant—it's a social enterprise that provides hospitality training and employment opportunities for underprivileged Rwandan youth.

Located in the heart of Kigali with stunning views of the city, Heaven serves a delightful fusion of international and Rwandan cuisine in a beautiful garden setting. Every meal you enjoy directly contributes to training the next generation of Rwanda's hospitality industry professionals.

Our Mission

Heaven operates as a social enterprise restaurant that trains at-risk youth in hospitality and culinary skills. Through comprehensive training programs, young Rwandans gain valuable work experience, professional skills, and confidence to build successful careers in the hospitality industry.

Over the past 15 years, Heaven has trained hundreds of young people, many of whom have gone on to successful careers in Rwanda's growing tourism sector. When you dine at Heaven, you're not just enjoying a delicious meal—you're investing in Rwanda's future.

Dining Tips

  1. Make a Reservation - Especially for weekend dinners and Sunday brunch, as Heaven is very popular with both locals and tourists.
  2. Try the Sunday Brunch - The buffet is legendary and offers great value with unlimited coffee and juice.
  3. Request Garden Seating - The outdoor terrace is the most beautiful spot, perfect for lunch or early dinner.
  4. Ask About Daily Specials - The kitchen often features special dishes based on fresh, seasonal ingredients.
  5. Visit the Gift Shop - Heaven sells beautiful handmade crafts, with proceeds supporting local artisans.
