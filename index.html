Quick Facts About Kigali

  • Population: Over 1.3 million people
  • Language: Kinyarwanda, French, English, and Swahili
  • Climate: Pleasant year-round with temperatures averaging 20-25°C
  • Currency: Rwandan Franc (RWF)
  • Known as: "The City of a Thousand Hills"
