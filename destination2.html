Welcome to Inema Arts Center

Inema Arts Center is East Africa's first art center, founded in 2012 by brothers Emmanuel Nkuranga and Innocent Nkurunziza. Located in the Kacyiru neighborhood of Kigali, Inema (meaning "happiness" in Kinyarwanda) has become a vibrant hub for contemporary African art, creativity, and cultural exchange.

The center showcases works from Rwanda's most talented artists alongside international exhibitions. With its colorful murals, open-air studios, and welcoming atmosphere, Inema has transformed from a simple art space into a cultural landmark that attracts art lovers, collectors, and tourists from around the world.

What You'll Experience

  • Contemporary Art Gallery: Rotating exhibitions featuring paintings, sculptures, and mixed media works by local and international artists
  • Open Studios: Watch artists at work and learn about their creative processes
  • Vibrant Murals: Instagram-worthy street art covering the center's walls
  • Art Workshops: Participate in painting, drawing, and craft workshops
  • Cultural Events: Live music performances, poetry readings, and artist talks
  • Gift Shop: Purchase original artworks and unique handmade crafts

Art Workshops & Classes

Inema Arts Center offers various creative workshops for all skill levels:

  1. Introduction to Painting - Learn basic painting techniques with acrylic and oil paints. Perfect for beginners. Duration: 2 hours.
  2. African Pattern Design - Discover traditional Rwandan patterns and create your own contemporary designs. Duration: 3 hours.
  3. Portrait Drawing - Master the fundamentals of facial proportions and shading techniques. Duration: 2.5 hours.
  4. Mixed Media Art - Experiment with various materials and techniques to create unique artworks. Duration: 3 hours.
  5. Children's Art Camp - Special weekend programs designed for young artists aged 6-14. Duration: Full day.

All workshops include materials and are taught by professional artists. Booking in advance is recommended.

Visitor Tips

  • Visit on First Friday evening for the best atmosphere with live music and refreshments
  • Bring cash if you plan to purchase artwork - not all vendors accept cards
  • Photography is welcome, but ask permission before photographing artists at work
  • Wear comfortable shoes - you'll want to explore all the outdoor murals
  • Allow at least 1-2 hours for your visit to fully appreciate the art
